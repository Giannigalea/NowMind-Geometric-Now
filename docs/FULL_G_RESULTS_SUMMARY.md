# Full-G Results Summary

This summary consolidates the frozen local evidence from G1 through G2.3.4. It is a research and engineering summary, not a consciousness claim.

## G1 - Present Geometry

Purpose: prove that each cognitive cycle can rebuild a fresh current relational state without reading previous Nows as cognitive input.

Experimental setup: deterministic symbolic world, current-cycle observation, Present Geometry construction, immutable `NowState`, and a reasoner that receives only the current `NowState` plus the query.

Headline result: stale-state contamination was `0`; query accuracy, inference accuracy, and contradiction detection were `1.0` in the G1 evidence suite.

Limitations: G1 is deterministic and symbolic. It is an architectural proof of a source boundary, not a superiority result and not a consciousness result.

What changed next: G2 added memory and future channels, but only as present reconstructions or hypotheses.

## G2 - Temporal Geometry

Purpose: separate present observation, reconstructed memory, and possible future content.

Experimental setup: synthetic temporal-source benchmark with NowMind Temporal Geometry, NaivePersistentState, and a strong ChronologicalRecordReasoner control.

Headline result: NowMind and Chronological both achieved `1.0` overall/source accuracy. NaivePersistentState failed badly with `0.332` overall accuracy, stale memory as current rate `0.502`, and prediction-as-fact rate `0.166`.

Limitations: source separation is necessary, but this benchmark does not show it is uniquely NowMind. A competent chronological control matched NowMind.

What changed next: G2.1 added spatial possibility planning and explicit hypothetical geometry.

## G2.1 - Possibility Geometry

Purpose: represent candidate future spatial transformations without treating them as current reality.

Experimental setup: deterministic grid planning with NowMind Possibility Geometry, Chronological Geometric Planner, Reactive Current-Only Planner, and Oracle.

Headline result: spatial possibility planning worked. NowMind goal reached rate was about `0.867`; Chronological and Reactive often matched it on this synthetic setup, while Oracle remained higher.

Limitations: temporal memory was mostly ornamental at this point. The benchmark did not isolate a strong NowMind-specific advantage over competent controls.

What changed next: G2.2 made observation partial and forced the system to reason under uncertainty and fog.

## G2.2 - Epistemic Geometry

Purpose: expose uncertainty, unknown cells, verification actions, and source-safe planning under partial observation.

Experimental setup: bounded sensor visibility, fog/unknown cells, SCAN information actions, memory-supported assumptions, and hidden-change cases.

Headline result: the pre-G2.2.1 baseline exposed real weaknesses: NowMind and Chronological goal reached rate was about `0.5`, hidden-change recovery was `0.0`, and NowMind inspected far more evidence than the chronological control.

Limitations: recovery and retrieval were not yet good enough. The result documented a problem, not a finished solution.

What changed next: G2.2.1 added recovery state and indexed retrieval while preserving source boundaries.

## G2.2.1 - Epistemic Recovery and Retrieval Efficiency

Purpose: recover from stale target memories and hidden obstacle changes, while making evidence counters comparable.

Experimental setup: v1 regression plus separate holdout, with indexed memory retrieval, disconfirmed target assumptions, invalidated free-cell assumptions, and recovery metrics.

Headline result: holdout NowMind goal reached rate was `0.9455`, hidden target recovery was `1.0`, hidden obstacle recovery was about `0.849`, and target reacquisition success was `1.0`. Reactive remained weaker with goal reached rate `0.7085` and hidden target recovery `0.0`.

Limitations: NowMind and Chronological remained tied on corrected retrieval and recovery metrics. Residual collisions and wasted verification remain visible.

What changed next: G2.3 introduced a replaceable model faculty and prompt/fairness infrastructure.

## G2.3 - Model Integration Infrastructure

Purpose: validate a strict representation-to-model-to-validator pipeline before relying on a real LLM.

Experimental setup: deterministic mock backend, NowMind structured representation, chronological representation, current-only representation, and no-LLM symbolic reference.

Headline result: the infrastructure worked. Mock N and C tied exactly in Regime A and Regime B across `1000` final paired trials, with prompt fairness failures `0`.

Limitations: this is mock-model evidence only. It validates the harness, parser, validator, and fairness checks, not real-model reasoning.

What changed next: G2.3.1/G2.3.2 ran and corrected the real local model benchmark.

## G2.3.1 and G2.3.2 - Real Local Model and Regime-B Fairness

Purpose: test the frozen representation benchmark with a real local model and then repair the Regime-B budget gate without changing the benchmark.

Experimental setup: local Ollama `qwen3:0.6b`, frozen 250 paired trials, Regime A equal information, and corrected Regime B fixed-token-budget enforcement.

Headline result: the real local benchmark completed. Regime A favored Chronological in `8` discordant cases, NowMind in `0`, ties `242`. Corrected Regime B tied all `250` pairs. No NowMind-over-chronology advantage was found.

Limitations: the model is very small. The Regime A chronological wins plausibly reflect tiny-model action/source-format weakness, but that remains an interpretation, not a proven mechanism.

What changed next: G2.3.3/G2.3.4 tested whether current exact-free OpenRouter models could provide a free cross-model replication.

## G2.3.3 and G2.3.4 - Free OpenRouter Replication Attempts

Purpose: attempt free cloud replication without paid models or benchmark changes.

Experimental setup: live exact `$0/$0` OpenRouter `:free` model discovery, exact model slugs, fallback disabled, provider pinning, strict schema, frozen scoring, and resumable rows.

Headline result: G2.3.3 was blocked under strict privacy/schema constraints. G2.3.4 relaxed only provider privacy routing for synthetic benchmark prompts; one candidate passed smoke but failed calibration, and other exact-free candidates were rate-limited, provider/parameter incompatible, or schema/provider invalid.

Limitations: no calibration-valid exact-free model was available under the frozen constraints. No cloud cross-model evidence was produced.

What changed next: model work is frozen for this milestone. The next useful research question is whether the unchanged benchmark behaves differently on a stronger free/local model or a later authorized non-paid runtime.
