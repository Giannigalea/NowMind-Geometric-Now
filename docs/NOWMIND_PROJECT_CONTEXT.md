# NowMind Project Context

## 1. What NowMind is

NowMind is an experimental AI architecture inspired by **Present Consciousness Theory (PCT)**.

The project explores whether cognition can be organized around a strict computational distinction between:

- what is represented **now**;
- information reconstructed from what happened before;
- hypotheses about what might happen;
- action selection in the present;
- continuity reconstructed across successive present states.

The project is a functional/research model. It does **not** assume or claim that implementing these mechanisms produces phenomenal consciousness.

## 2. PCT origin

PCT proposes that conscious experience is always present-bound. The apparent flow through time is constructed by cognition by connecting successive present moments.

A useful computational translation is:

- the current processing state is primary;
- the past can affect the present only through information available now;
- a remembered event is a present reconstruction, not a return to a former conscious moment;
- an anticipated future is a present hypothesis, not an experienced future;
- continuity is a useful reconstruction across Nows rather than a literal conscious object travelling through time.

The "Waterfall" perspective is a key metaphor: the experienced present is analogous to the active edge/location of the waterfall. The water/process continues, but the currently active form is always the present configuration.

The implementation must turn this metaphor into explicit data boundaries and testable rules.

## 3. Prior NowMind principles that remain relevant

Earlier NowMind work explored:
- a "baby AI" / developmental architecture;
- perception-first processing;
- memory as a tool rather than the location of identity;
- fuzzy/cue-triggered reconstruction rather than exact replay;
- continuity stitching across Nows;
- evolving goals with external positive/negative feedback;
- a dreaming mode based only on prior internal experience when external perception is unavailable;
- a layered architecture with L1 Substrate, L2 Conscious Core, and L3 Veto Gate;
- action inhibition under uncertainty as a safety mechanism;
- identity drift/coherence checks.

Not all of these belong in G1. They are roadmap context only.

## 4. Why introduce geometry

"Geometry" in this project does not merely mean Euclidean geometry or solving triangle problems.

It means that the current state can be represented as a structured relational object:

- entities are nodes/objects;
- relations are typed edges/constraints;
- spatial quantities may be coordinates, distance, orientation, containment, contact, order, etc.;
- non-spatial relations may be introduced in later generations;
- confidence and provenance are explicit;
- reasoning is performed over the structure of the current state.

Thus a Now becomes a **Present Geometry**.

## 5. Distinction from latent/model geometry

This project must distinguish two meanings of geometric reasoning:

### A. Internal latent geometry
Research groups may study the geometry of neural representations inside trained models.

### B. NowMind Present Geometry
NowMind explicitly constructs a relational geometry representing the current cognitive/world state and reasons over transformations/relations in that geometry.

These ideas may eventually intersect, but they are not assumed to be equivalent.

## 6. The key G1 research question

Can we implement a cognitive runtime in which:

> every reasoning operation is grounded only in a newly constructed relational representation of the current observable state, with no previous cognitive state silently carried forward?

G1 is about proving that this architectural discipline is technically possible and measurable.

## 7. What G1 deliberately excludes

G1 does not attempt to implement:
- autobiographical memory;
- identity continuity;
- future simulation;
- ethics/veto;
- dreaming;
- emotional valuation;
- language-model reasoning;
- embodiment through real cameras/microphones;
- consciousness measurement;
- quantum mechanisms.

Those layers follow only after the Present Geometry foundation is reliable.

## 8. Long-term hypothesis

The broader NowMind hypothesis is that a system may gain useful improvements in:
- temporal state separation;
- object/state consistency;
- interpretability;
- resistance to false-memory contamination;
- identity continuity without equating identity with stored memory;
- action safety through explicit candidate-state evaluation;

when past, present, and possible future are represented as distinct structures that are brought into the present under explicit rules rather than blended into one undifferentiated context.

This is a hypothesis to test, not a conclusion.
