## What Was Built

NowMind Full-G is a local Python research architecture for explicit present-state reconstruction and source-aware reasoning. It implements stages from G1 through G2.3.4: Present Geometry, Temporal Geometry, Possibility Geometry, Epistemic Geometry, recovery/retrieval efficiency, model-integration infrastructure, local real-model evaluation, and frozen free-provider replication attempts.

The project is deliberately inspectable. Relations, observations, inferences, reconstructed memories, hypothetical futures, model proposals, validator decisions, and external researcher history are kept separate.

## Why "Geometry"

"Geometry" here means explicit relational structure, not a claim about phenomenal consciousness. A current state is reconstructed as a structured space of entities, relations, sources, confidence, and possible transformations. The point is to make state provenance visible and auditable.

## Present Geometry vs Latent Geometry

Present Geometry is symbolic and explicit. It records facts such as current observation, inferred relation, reconstructed memory, or hypothetical future.

Latent/internal geometry in neural systems is implicit and distributed. The possible research intersection is whether explicit source-labeled geometry can become a useful interface to, diagnostic probe for, or training target for latent reasoning geometry. The current project does not prove that connection.

## Experimental Progression

G1 proves fresh current-state reconstruction with no stale `NowState` input.

G2 adds memory and future source separation.

G2.1 adds spatial possibility planning.

G2.2 adds partial observation and verification.

G2.2.1 repairs hidden-target/hidden-obstacle recovery and retrieval accounting.

G2.3 adds model comparison infrastructure.

G2.3.1/G2.3.2 run and repair the local `qwen3:0.6b` real-model benchmark.

G2.3.3/G2.3.4 attempt exact-free OpenRouter replication and stop under frozen gates.

## Strongest Findings

The architecture can enforce fresh-state and source-separation invariants across increasingly complex synthetic tasks. Memory can be reconstructed without replaying old Nows. Future hypotheses can guide planning without becoming observation. Epistemic recovery can disconfirm stale assumptions while preserving historical memory traces.

## Negative Results

Strong chronological controls frequently match NowMind. The local `qwen3:0.6b` model did not show a NowMind accuracy advantage: Regime A favored Chronological 8-0 among discordant cases, and corrected Regime B tied all 250 pairs. Free OpenRouter models did not reach calibration-valid replication under frozen constraints.

## Real-Model Limitation

The only completed real-model benchmark is one very small local model. The result should be treated as weak model evidence and strong infrastructure evidence.
