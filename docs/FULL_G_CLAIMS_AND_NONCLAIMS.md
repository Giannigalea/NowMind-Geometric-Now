# Full-G Claims and Nonclaims

## Allowed Claims

- NowMind can enforce fresh-state reconstruction without stale-state carryover in the implemented G1 runtime.
- Temporal source separation can prevent stale memory or prediction from being treated as present fact.
- Possibility Geometry can represent candidate future states without promoting them to observation.
- Epistemic Geometry can represent unknown cells, conditional assumptions, verification actions, replanning, and recovery.
- The architecture is inspectable and source-aware: observed, inferred, reconstructed-memory, and hypothetical-future content are distinguished.
- External experiment history can be retained for evaluation without becoming runtime cognition.
- Strong symbolic controls show that many benefits are not unique to the NowMind representation.
- Real-model evidence from local `qwen3:0.6b` does not show a NowMind reasoning advantage over chronology.
- Free OpenRouter replication attempts produced useful infrastructure and stop-condition evidence, but no calibration-valid cross-model replication.

## Forbidden Claims

Do not claim that this project proves:

- consciousness;
- sentience;
- phenomenal awareness;
- identity continuity;
- model superiority in general;
- quantum consciousness;
- that NowMind is a person;
- that NowMind has rights or feelings;
- that NowMind outperforms conventional AI generally;
- that the G2.3 model result supports a NowMind-over-chronology advantage.

## Preferred Framing

The honest claim is architectural: explicit present-state reconstruction and source provenance can be implemented, audited, benchmarked, and stress-tested. The evidence so far supports source discipline and inspectability, while also showing that competent chronological controls often match the architecture on current synthetic tasks.
